# Week 4 — Reviews

**The whole week in one sentence:** once this week, spend 10 minutes turning your daily chaos into compound interest.

## The weekly review

Pick a consistent slot (Sunday evening and Friday afternoon are the classics). Then:

1. Create a note from the [[weekly review]] template (`Ctrl/Cmd + P` → Insert template)
2. Skim the week's notes in `00-daily/` — with the Calendar plugin this takes two minutes
3. Fill in the four sections: what happened, open loops, anything worth promoting, one focus

## "Promoting" — the step that makes this a system

Most daily-note content should rot in peace. But a few things deserve to graduate:

- A recurring thought → becomes a note in `20-ideas/`
- A useful link or explanation → `30-reference/`
- A "we should build X" → `10-projects/`

The daily note is the inbox; the review is the sort. This is the entire engine — capture with zero friction, organise once a week with ten minutes of hindsight.
