# Week 1 — The Daily Note

**The whole week in one sentence:** open today's note once a day and write *something* in it.

## Why this first

Every abandoned Obsidian vault died the same way: an elaborate system, no habit. The daily note flips that — it's one habit with zero decisions. No "where does this note go?" paralysis. Everything goes in today's note. Sorting comes later (week 4), and most things never need sorting at all.

## How

1. `Ctrl/Cmd + P` → **"Daily notes: Open today's daily note"** (this vault is pre-configured — it lands in `00-daily/`, pre-filled from [[daily]])
2. Write under whichever heading fits: what happened, a todo, a stray thought
3. Close it. Done.

**Pro move:** pin the command, or click the calendar-ish "Open today's daily note" ribbon icon on the left edge.

## What counts as "something"

- One sentence of diary: *"Shipped the login fix, felt great."*
- One todo: `- [ ] cancel that subscription`
- One complaint. Complaints are excellent note content.

## What NOT to do this week

- ❌ Install plugins
- ❌ Reorganise folders
- ❌ Watch Obsidian YouTube (a genuinely dangerous rabbit hole)

## Graduation criteria

Five daily notes exist. That's it. Then move to [[Week 2 - Links]].
