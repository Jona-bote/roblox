# 👋 Start here

Welcome. You're inside a vault that teaches itself.

## Right now (2 minutes)

1. Look at the **left sidebar** — that's your file explorer. Poke around, you can't break anything.
2. Press `Ctrl/Cmd + P` — that's the **command palette**. Every Obsidian action lives here. It's the only shortcut you *must* know.
3. Click this link: [[Week 1 - The Daily Note]] — notice how it just... opens? That's a wikilink. You'll be making hundreds of these.

## Your only job this week

Open the command palette and run **"Daily notes: Open today's daily note"**. A note appears in `00-daily/`, pre-filled from a template. Write literally anything in it:

- What you did today
- What's annoying you
- A todo like `- [ ] buy milk`
- A half-formed idea

Do that once a day. That's the entire week 1 curriculum. The rest of the onboarding path is in the `onboarding/` folder, one week per note:

- [[Week 1 - The Daily Note]]
- [[Week 2 - Links]]
- [[Week 3 - Tasks and Plugins]]
- [[Week 4 - Reviews]]
